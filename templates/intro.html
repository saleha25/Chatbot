About Us

Built for
Pakistan's IDPs

Empath AI was created in response to Pakistan's growing internally displaced population — millions of people who have been forced from their homes by floods, conflict, and climate crises, and who carry invisible wounds alongside everything else.

We are a team of researchers, mental health professionals, and technologists united by one belief: no one should face trauma alone. Our platform combines clinically validated screening tools — PHQ-9 and GAD-7 — with culturally sensitive conversation design, so every assessment feels human, not clinical.

Empath AI speaks your language, understands your context, and connects you to real support when it matters most. We are private, safe, and always available — because mental health care should have no waiting room.
