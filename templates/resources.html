Lost ID card (NADRA)

CNIC, B-form and other identity documents

NADRA helpline (mobile)
From any mobile network.
1777
NADRA helpline (landline)
From a landline phone.
051-111-786-100
Lost your CNIC? Go to a NADRA Registration Centre with your CNIC number. A police report (FIR) is not required. Keep your CNIC number written on paper or saved in your phone.

Simple steps that help

Small things that make the next days easier

1

Write important numbers on paper too, in case your phone is lost or the battery dies.

2

Keep photos of your CNIC, B-forms and important papers, and send a copy to a family member you trust.

3

Ask the camp management or district office how to register for relief, and keep any token or SMS you receive.

4

Government help is free. Do not pay anyone who promises to "speed it up".

5

Look after your mind too: a short exercise in the Calm Corner or a chat can help on hard days.

Numbers checked on 16 November 2026. Services can change. If a number does not work in an emergency, call 1122 or 15. Empath AI is not part of these organisations.
